Anatomy of a Tribunal case

How I run your case, start to finish.

01 / 08
STAGE
01DAY ONE

The first conversation

A confidential call to understand what happened, what you want, and how strong your position is.

I take this call myself.
02WITHIN TIME LIMITS

ACAS early conciliation

Every claim starts here. It pauses the clock and opens the first window for settlement.

I set the strategy for any early negotiation.
03THE CLAIM

The ET1 claim form

Your claim is set out precisely, because what is pleaded now shapes everything that follows.

I draft the claim with the final hearing in mind.
04CASE MANAGEMENT

Preliminary hearing

The Tribunal fixes the issues, the timetable and the length of the final hearing.

I attend and argue the issues for you.
05EVIDENCE

Disclosure and the bundle

Documents from both sides are exchanged. The emails your employer would rather keep quiet often surface here.

I know every page of the bundle.
06YOUR ACCOUNT

Witness statements

Your story, told clearly and in order, in a form the Tribunal can rely on.

I prepare you for what will be asked.
07THE HEARING

Cross-examination

From a one-day hearing to a thirty-day trial, this is where cases are won.

I cross-examine your employer's witnesses.
08OUTCOME

Judgment and remedy

Compensation, a declaration, or a settlement negotiated from a position of strength.

I see it through to the end.

Working with me

The questions clients ask first.

Will you be at my hearing?+

Yes. I will be the one presenting your case and cross-examining your employer's witnesses. You will not meet a new barrister the week before trial.

How quickly can we speak?+

Usually within one working day. Choose a callback time below and I will call you then. If your time limit is close, call 0333 242 3851 straight away.

Do you work with legal expenses insurers?+

Yes. Many professionals have cover through a home or bank policy without realising it. I will help you check, and work with your insurer directly.

What will it cost?+

Fees are agreed in writing before any work starts, with a clear estimate for each stage of the claim. [Fee information to be supplied.]

Will my employer know I have contacted you?+

No. Everything you tell me is confidential. Many of my clients are still employed when we first speak.
