Important: time limits

Most Employment Tribunal claims must be started within three months less one day of the act complained about or the date of dismissal. Before you can file a claim you must notify ACAS, the free government conciliation service; the clock pauses while you are in early conciliation. It is your responsibility to make sure your claim is filed on time, whether or not we act for you.
